JWTs (JSON Web Tokens) are the small encoded strings that web apps use to prove you're logged in — they're embedded in cookies, URLs, and API headers. `jwtinspect` lets you paste one of these tokens into a command and instantly see what's inside it: who issued it, when it expires, and whether it was signed with a dangerously weak secret or a misconfigured algorithm that lets attackers forge their own tokens. It's aimed at developers and security testers who want a fast, offline check without uploading sensitive tokens to a website.
